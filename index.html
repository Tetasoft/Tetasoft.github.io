About Tetasoft

Founded in 2003, Tetasoft A/S is an independent Danish SAP consultancy specialising in solution architecture and full-stack development on SAP S/4HANA and SAP BTP.

We work as an integrated part of our clients' teams on greenfield implementations, modernisation and migration projects, side-by-side extensions and integrations. We stay involved from the first architecture decisions through to stable operation.

Our work follows SAP's clean-core direction and uses SAP's latest extensibility and AI capabilities. We deliver maintainable, well-documented solutions and share the knowledge, so our clients' own teams can take over with confidence.
